# SEO setup

The site now includes:
- Birmingham-focused page titles and meta descriptions
- `index, follow` robots directives
- Open Graph and Twitter metadata
- LocalBusiness/Plumber JSON-LD with the company address, phone, email and company number
- Location-focused H1 headings
- A Birmingham-focused homepage introduction
- `robots.txt`

## SEO maintenance

When the final website domain is confirmed, update the canonical and Open Graph URLs and submit the sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

Do not add a `meta keywords` tag; Google does not use it for ranking.
